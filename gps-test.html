How to test
  1. Stand on the street itself, at the kerb, on the north-west side (toward Mount Royal). Wait ~30 seconds and read the side + confidence.
  2. Cross to the south-east side (the 1558 side), same thing, ~30 seconds.
  3. Compare: does the page say NW on the NW side and SE on the SE side — and does it stay stable, or flip?
  4. Ignore readings taken from inside the house or on the sidewalk: the house is ~25 m from the centreline, so that side is trivially easy and proves nothing.
  5. The street is only ~10 m wide (parked cars sit ~3.5 m from the centre). A reported accuracy bigger than ~5 m means the phone itself is telling you it cannot tell the sides apart.
