What never happens to your files

  • Your files are never uploaded to a server — processing happens in your browser's memory.
  • No account, no sign-up, no email address — nothing to create, nothing to log into.
  • Your files aren't stored anywhere: close the tab and they're gone from the tool.
  • Free means ads: JSM Image and JSM Video show Google ads, and the sites use anonymous Google Analytics statistics. That keeps them free — it doesn't change the promise above.
  • One honest exception: JSM Enhance's AI tools upload your image to a server for AI processing. That's why only its four in-browser tools are listed here.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers regarding functionality and privacy.

Do these tools upload my files?

No. Every tool listed on this page was checked before listing: your files are read and processed in your browser, and nothing is sent to a server. The one exception to know about is JSM Enhance's AI tools (Extend, Sharpen, Unblur and Remove) — they upload your image to a server for AI processing, which is why they are not in the list above.

Is it really free — what's the catch?

Yes, every tool listed here is free to use. The sites stay free with Google ads (on JSM Image and JSM Video) and anonymous usage statistics. There is no account to create and no paywall on the listed tools.

Do I need an account?

No. None of these tools asks you to sign in or give an email address. JSM Enhance's AI tools use pay-per-use credits bought with a code — still no account — but the four Enhance tools listed here are free and run in your browser.

Can I use them offline?

No. The tools run in your browser, but you need to load the page first — they don't work offline.

Which tools are included?

22 tools: 7 image tools, 4 video tools, 4 audio tools, 4 in-browser photo tools from JSM Enhance, 2 options tools, and LyricPrep for lyrics. Every one of them processes your files — or your text, in LyricPrep's case — on your own device.

What about JSM Enhance's AI tools?

Extend, Sharpen, Unblur and Remove use AI running on a server, so your image is uploaded to process it. They are good tools, but they don't belong on a no-upload list — only Enhance's four in-browser tools (AutoFix, Adjust, Watermark, Add Text) are listed above.
