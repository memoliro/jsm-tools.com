Location
GPS can’t tell the sides apart on this street (the two parking rows are only ~7 m apart) — that’s why you tap. This confirms the street, not the side. Your choice is saved on this device only.
Data: City of Montréal open data, Stationnement sur rue — signalisation courante (CC-BY 4.0), as of —. Joined to street segment ID_TRC 1260585 (Rue McGregor to Chemin de la Côte-des-Neiges) from signs 3–10 m off the road centreline; a window counts as established only when two or more poles carry the identical sign. Season Apr 1 – Dec 1 inclusive. Arrow codes on the signs (FLECHE_PAN) are not decoded in the open data, so no arrow is assumed to mean both directions. Centreline: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Times are America/Toronto.
Cleaning only. Winter snow removal is separate — infoneige.ca already covers it. No-stopping panels (\A EN TOUT TEMPS) are a stopping ban, not a cleaning window.
Resident permits: the photographed SE sign also carries “EXCEPTÉ VÉHICULES MUNIS D’UN PERMIS, SECTEUR 41” — that plate exempts the 2-hour limit only, not the cleaning ban; during the cleaning window permit holders must still move the car.
